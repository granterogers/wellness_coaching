# The offer

## In one sentence

A private, personalised 1:1 coaching programme, delivered online over three to six months, that helps busy, capable people build a health routine that fits their real life and keeps working when life gets demanding.

## Positioning

**Science-informed mind-body wellness coaching.** A scientist turned practitioner: a background in physics and biology combined with practical experience teaching yoga, breathwork, meditation, Yoga Nidra and movement.

## Who it is for

Intelligent, analytical people, roughly late 30s to early 50s, men and women alike, often in technology, science, engineering, finance or other demanding fields. They know what they should do but can't make it stick. They may be dealing with low energy, poor sleep, stress they can't switch off, stiffness from sitting, lost fitness or changes in sexual wellbeing. They are sceptical of hype and spiritual language, but open to body-based practices when the reasoning is clear.

## Pillars

The pillars are a flexible draft, kept in `pillars-draft.md` so they can change without touching the design.

## How the programme runs

1. **Baseline** — goals, schedule and constraints, sleep, stress, current habits, any reasons for medical referral.
2. **Stabilise** — sleep and evening wind-down, a short daily regulation practice, basic movement.
3. **Build capacity** — strength, fitness, mobility, deeper practice, vitality.
4. **Integrate** — a personal routine the client owns, plus plans for travel and busy periods.

Between sessions: personalised routines, short video demonstrations, guided recordings and music, practice plans and accountability check-ins.

## Programme rhythm (Grant, 4 November 2026)

- **Sessions:** 1:1 by video. Length and frequency are set for each client's situation and needs; no fixed schedule is promised on the site.
- **Between sessions:** clients get in touch as needed; no fixed check-in schedule.
- **Discovery call ("Book a conversation"):** free, 30 minutes. We talk through goals, schedule and anything a doctor should see first. If it's a fit, Grant emails a written plan and the investment within two days. No pressure on the call.
- **Languages and time zones:** English or Spanish; clients anywhere in the world, with morning and evening slots across time zones.

## What makes it different

- **Evidence-informed.** Practices are explained, and clients learn what is well supported, what is plausible, and what is simply experiential.
- **Minimum effective dose.** Routines are designed to survive the busiest week, sometimes as short as five minutes.
- **Truly individual.** Built around each client's goals, body, schedule and preferences.
- **Body and mind together.** Movement, breath, attention and relaxation in one approach.
- **No dogma.** Traditional practices without requiring any belief system.
- **Independence.** The aim is for clients to run their own practice without needing a coach.

## Not on the website

- Named sub-programmes (Calm & Resilience, Energy & Vitality, and so on). These are concepts, not delivered programmes.
- Wim Hof-style or hypoxic breathing as standard methods.
- Any claim about hormones, testosterone, specific glands or guaranteed sexual-function outcomes.
