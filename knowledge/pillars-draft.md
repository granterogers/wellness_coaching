Origin: five pillars come from Grant's content document (Breathe, Move, Focus, Restore, Grow). Vitality was added to give sexual wellbeing a discreet place.

1. **Breathe** — breath awareness, relaxation and physiological self-regulation.
2. **Move** — yoga, mobility, flexibility, strength and physical awareness.
3. **Focus** — meditation, mindfulness and attention training.
4. **Restore** — deep relaxation, Yoga Nidra, sleep-supportive habits and recovery.
5. **Vitality** — body awareness, pelvic awareness and sexual wellbeing, within a non-clinical coaching scope.
6. **Grow** — self-understanding, sustainable habits and independence.
