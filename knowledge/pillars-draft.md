# Pillars

Status: decided by Grant, 10 November 2026 — four pillars, named after what changes for the client (from the interviews: sleep, strength, a mind that won't switch off, tiredness). The site loads pillars from content (`content/en/home.md`), so they can still be renamed, merged, added or removed without changing code (requirement 3.3).

1. **Sleep** — sleep, deep rest and recovery, Yoga Nidra.
2. **Strength** — mobility, strength, fitness and yoga; keeping performing with age.
3. **Calm** — breathwork, meditation and attention, nervous-system regulation in plain words.
4. **Energy** — energy, body confidence, pelvic awareness and sexual wellbeing, within a non-clinical coaching scope.

Earlier draft (six): Breathe, Move, Focus, Restore, Vitality, Grow. "Grow" (habits and independence) now lives in the programme's Integrate phase.
