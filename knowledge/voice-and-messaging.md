# Voice and messaging

## Tone

Calm rather than sensational. Intelligent without being academic. Direct without being aggressive. Practical, private and respectful, especially on sexual wellbeing. Slightly challenging. Speak to visitors as capable adults, not as broken people.

## Core message

> You don't need another pile of health information. You need a personalised system that improves your energy, strength, recovery and wellbeing, and survives the demands of your real life.

## Headline options

- A more intelligent approach to wellbeing.
- Science meets self-awareness.
- Wellness designed around your actual life.
- Understand your body. Train your mind. Build habits that last.

## Words that resonate

Evidence-informed, practical, personalised, measurable, sustainable, recovery, mobility, energy, body awareness, nervous-system regulation, minimum effective dose, designed around your life.

## Words to avoid or define carefully

"Raise your vibration", "divine masculine/feminine", "manifest", "tantric energy", "detox", "balance your hormones naturally", "quantum".

## Objections the site should quietly answer

| Visitor thinks | Site answers |
|---|---|
| "I already know what to do." | We focus on implementation and consistency, not information. |
| "I don't have time." | We start with the smallest routine that survives your busiest week. |
| "I'm sceptical of yoga and breathwork." | No belief system required; every practice comes with its reasoning. |
| "I can do this myself." | That's the goal: a system you can eventually run alone. |
| "Is this medical treatment?" | No. Coaching complements clinical care and refers on when needed. |
