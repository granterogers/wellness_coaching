# Photo slots

Every photo on the site has a slot. Until Grant supplies the real photo, the site shows a **descriptive placeholder**: a panel in the palette's texture tone with the slot's brief (below) set in mono type, e.g. "FIG. 01 — PORTRAIT · SOLO · MEDIUM CLOSE-UP · BY A WINDOW, MORNING LIGHT · LOOKING JUST OFF CAMERA". No stock photos, no grey boxes.

To replace: save the photo in `src/assets/photos/` with the slot's file name. The site picks it up automatically.

General direction (all slots): natural light, real places, slightly warm colour grade, plain tonal clothing (ink, stone, olive; no logos), calm and unposed unless stated. Shoot horizontal **and** vertical where marked.
