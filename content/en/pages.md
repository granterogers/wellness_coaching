# Programme

**Page title:** The programme — Grant Rogers

**Meta description:** Four phases over three to six months, online and one to one: what happens in each, what's included, and what coaching does and doesn't cover.

**Heading:** How the programme works

**Intro:** One flagship programme, delivered online over three to six months. No two run the same way, because they're built around your goals, your body, your schedule and what you'll actually do.

## Who it's for

Capable, analytical people, often in demanding jobs in technology, science, engineering or finance, men and women alike. You know what you should be doing. Sleep, stress, stiffness from sitting, lost fitness or changes in how your body feels have crept in anyway, and the plans you've tried haven't survived real life. You're sceptical of hype, and open to body-based practice when the reasoning is clear.

## The four phases

### Phase 01 / Baseline
We start with where you are, not where a plan says you should be: your goals, your schedule and constraints, sleep, stress, current habits, and anything that should go to a doctor first.

### Phase 02 / Stabilise
Sleep and evening wind-down first, then a short daily practice and basic movement. Everything is sized to survive your busiest week. Sometimes that's five minutes.

### Phase 03 / Build capacity
Once the basics hold, we add strength, fitness, mobility and deeper practice. Only now does the work get harder, and only as far as your week allows.

### Phase 04 / Integrate
You leave with a routine you own, and a plan for travel, illness, busy periods and the weeks when everything goes wrong.

Phase lengths depend on you. Some people need longer in Stabilise; some move through quickly.

## How it works week to week

We meet one to one by video. How often, and for how long, is set around your situation and what you need, and it changes as the programme moves on. Between sessions you get in touch whenever you need to: a question, a bad week, something that's working better than expected.

## What's included

- Personalised routines, written for your week
- Short video demonstrations of each practice
- Guided recordings and music
- Practice plans for each phase
- Check-ins whenever you need them

## What coaching covers, and what it doesn't

**Covers:** habits and routine; movement, fitness and mobility; practices for stress; sleep-supportive habits; meditation and relaxation; body awareness, including pelvic awareness; accountability.

**Doesn't cover:** diagnosing or treating any condition; rehabilitation; hormone or medication advice; psychotherapy; promises of specific physical or sexual outcomes.

If something you tell me needs a clinician, I'll say so, and our work will sit alongside theirs.

# About

**Page title:** About — Grant Rogers

**Meta description:** Two MSc degrees, seven peer-reviewed papers and fifteen years across science, GIS and technical training, alongside yoga, breathwork and meditation teaching since 2020.

**Heading:** Scientist turned practitioner

## Story

For more than fifteen years I worked in science, GIS and technical training. I studied computational physics, then marine biodiversity and biotechnology, both at Heriot-Watt University. I co-authored seven peer-reviewed papers, analysed large-scale human movement from satellite and mobile data at the University of Southampton, and taught technical skills in more than forty workshops for international clients.

Alongside that, I trained as a Kundalini yoga teacher with the i-Sky Institute and as a SOMA Breathwork instructor in Ibiza, and became a CMAS 3-star freediver, which teaches you a great deal about breath. I've taught since 2020: private one-to-one sessions, group classes, retreats, festivals and online courses.

The science taught me to ask what's actually supported. Teaching taught me what people will actually do. The coaching sits between the two.
