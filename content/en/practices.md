# Breath pacer

**Length:** 1 minute · six breaths, in for 4 seconds, out for 6

- [0:00] Let's take a minute together. Breathe in through your nose…
- [0:04] …and let it go, slowly, for six.
- [0:10] Again. In for four…
- [0:14] …and out for six, a little longer than feels natural.
- [0:20] In…
- [0:24] …notice your shoulders. Let them drop.
- [0:30] In, through the nose…
- [0:34] …and a long, slow breath out.
- [0:40] One more like that. In…
- [0:44] …and all the way out.
- [0:50] Last one. In…
- [0:54] …and out. That's it. That's the whole practice. You can do it anywhere.

# Body scan

**Length:** about 2 minutes · settle for 10 seconds, then 5 seconds on each part, then 10 seconds on the whole body

- [0:00] Lie down, or sit back. Let your body be still. As I name each part, let your attention rest there. Nothing to do.
- [0:10] Right hand.
- [0:15] Right forearm.
- [0:20] Right upper arm.
- [0:25] Right shoulder.
- [0:30] Right hip.
- [0:35] Right thigh.
- [0:40] Right lower leg.
- [0:45] Right foot. The sole, the toes.
- [0:50] Left hand.
- [0:55] Left forearm.
- [1:00] Left upper arm.
- [1:05] Left shoulder.
- [1:10] Left hip.
- [1:15] Left thigh.
- [1:20] Left lower leg.
- [1:25] Left foot. The sole, the toes.
- [1:30] The top of the head.
- [1:35] The face. Forehead, eyes, jaw.
- [1:40] The throat.
- [1:45] The chest, rising and falling.
- [1:50] The belly.
- [1:55] The pelvis.
- [2:00] The whole body. The whole body, resting. Stay here as long as you like. When you're ready, move your fingers and toes.
