# Privacy

**Page title:** Privacy — Grant Rogers

**Meta description:** What this site and the coaching collect about you, why, and your rights under UK data protection law.

## Who is responsible

Grant Rogers is responsible for personal data collected through this site and the coaching (the "controller" under UK GDPR). [[PLACEHOLDER: Legal details — trading name and postal address for the privacy notice, and whether you are registered with the ICO]]

To ask anything about your data, message me on WhatsApp using the link at the bottom of the home page.

## What this site collects

- **Nothing through forms.** There is no contact form and no newsletter sign-up.
- **No cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.**
- **Your choices, on your own device.** If you switch between light and dark, the choice is saved in your browser's storage so the next page matches. When you switch language, your position on the page is kept for that one move. Neither is sent to me or anyone else.
- **Server logs.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to run and protect the service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

## When you book or message me

- **Booking.** Online booking uses Calendly. The calendar loads only when you choose to show it. If you book, Calendly collects your name, email address and anything you write in the booking form, and sends it to me.
- **WhatsApp.** If you message me, WhatsApp (Meta) handles the message under its own terms, and I see your name and number as WhatsApp shows them.

## During coaching

To coach you safely I need information about your goals, routine, sleep, stress and, where relevant, your health. Health information is special category data: I only ask for what the coaching needs, and I use it only to coach you, with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

I keep notes from our sessions and the plans I write for you. [[PLACEHOLDER: Retention — how long you keep client notes and booking details after coaching ends, and where they are stored]]

I don't sell your data or share it for marketing. I share it only if you ask me to (for example, with your doctor) or if the law requires it.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the data I hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how I use it, and to receive a copy to take elsewhere. Message me and I'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). I'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be on this page with its date.
