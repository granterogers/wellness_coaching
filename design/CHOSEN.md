# Chosen prototype

**`design/prototype-d.html`** — chosen by Grant, 6 November 2026.

- Light mode = palette A, Sage & Saffron. Dark mode = palette C, Moss & Honey (`knowledge/design-system.md`, round 2).
- The site follows the visitor's device setting by default; a clearly labelled Light/Dark button in the header lets them switch, remembered on that device (requirement 6.9).
- Layout as in the prototype: offer-first hero with the breath pacer, "How this is different", pillars side by side (swipe row on mobile), simple left-to-right programme timeline, night final CTA, quiet footer with the full logo.
- Grant's round 1 notes still apply: warm, not clinical; no noise (no grid textures, figure captions, section eyebrows or decorative diagrams); easy-to-follow reading order.
- Sections the prototype doesn't show (Recognition, About teaser, FAQ, voice clip) follow the same rules.
- To add later: mini Yoga Nidra body scan with the Restore pillar (requirement 9.13).
