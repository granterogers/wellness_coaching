How this is different

  1. 1

    Evidence, not woo

    Every practice comes with its reason. You'll know what's well supported, what's plausible, and what's simply worth trying. No belief system required.

  2. 2

    Built around you

    Your goals, your body, your schedule. Nothing comes off the shelf, and the plan changes when your life does.

  3. 3

    Small enough to last

    A five-minute practice you do on your worst day beats an hour you skip. We build up from what survives, until you can run it without me.

Six areas we draw on

You won't work on all six at once. We start where it matters most for you, and add the rest when the basics hold.

Swipe to see all 6 →

  1. 01

    Breathe

    Slower breathing you can use anywhere: before a meeting, in traffic, at 3am.

    TryFour seconds in, six seconds out, for one minute.

    Longer exhales tend to shift the body toward rest, which is why we usually start here.

  2. 02

    Move

    Mobility, strength and yoga for a body that sits all day.

    TryA ten-minute floor routine before your first coffee.

    For stiff hips and backs, a little every day tends to do more than a long session once a week.

  3. 03

    Focus

    Meditation and attention training, with no incense required.

    TryThree minutes counting breaths at your desk, eyes open.

    Short, regular practice is easier to keep than long sessions, and the keeping is what counts.

  4. 04

    Restore

    Sleep, deep rest and recovery, including Yoga Nidra.

    TryA guided Yoga Nidra recording for the evenings your mind won't switch off.

    It gives a busy mind something to follow instead of something to fight.

  5. 05

    Vitality

    Energy, confidence in your body, and sexual wellbeing, talked about privately, plainly and within coaching scope.

    TryPelvic-floor awareness built into your movement routine.

    It's part of the same body you train everywhere else, so it belongs in the same routine. Anything clinical goes to a doctor.

  6. 06

    Grow

    Habits that last, and a practice that's yours.

    TryA ten-minute weekly review: what worked, what didn't, what we change.

    Small changes every week keep a routine alive when your life changes around it.

Three to six months, in four phases

Online and one to one. How often we meet depends on your situation and what you need. Between sessions, you get in touch whenever you need to.

  1. Phase 01

    Baseline

    Where you are now: your goals, schedule and constraints, sleep, stress, current habits, and anything a doctor should look at first.

  2. Phase 02

    Stabilise

    Sleep and evening wind-down, a short daily practice and basic movement. Small enough to survive your busiest week.

  3. Phase 03

    Build capacity

    Strength, fitness, mobility and deeper practice, once the basics hold.

  4. Phase 04

    Integrate

    A routine you own, with plans for travel and busy periods.
